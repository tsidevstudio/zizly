# Zizly Recording Guide

Zizly is designed for sleep-talk recording and later review.

## Before recording

- Confirm microphone permission is enabled.
- Confirm sufficient local storage is available.
- Review device battery and background restrictions.

## Recording environment

Recording quality depends on device microphone quality, distance, background noise and room conditions.

## Privacy

Treat recordings as private audio.

Review recordings before sharing them and store exported copies only in locations you trust.

## About this documentation

This document covers public, user-facing behavior only.

It does not publish internal recording logic, audio-processing implementation or private app architecture.
